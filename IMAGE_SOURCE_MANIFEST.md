# KAYAM SPICES — Image source and licence manifest

Last updated: 28 October 2026

## Usage and licence

Stock images in this manifest are sourced from Pexels. The individual Pexels pages identify each source as a free stock photo or free-to-use image. The Pexels licence is available at https://www.pexels.com/license/ and permits free commercial use subject to its terms and third-party rights. Attribution is not generally required, but the photographer/source is recorded here for traceability.

The live site requests resized/compressed images from the Pexels CDN using `auto=compress`, `cs=tinysrgb` and an appropriate `w=` parameter. Image delivery therefore depends on the external Pexels CDN. The photos are food/ingredient context images, not photos of KAYAM-branded retail packaging, and do not confirm ingredients or the formula of a particular KAYAM product.
