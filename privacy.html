Your information

Privacy policy.

This policy explains what information KAYAM SPICES collects through this website, why it is needed, and how you can contact us about it.

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Who we are

KAYAM SPICES operates this website and manages orders placed through it. For privacy questions, contact us at anikhindi.suchi@gmail.com or on WhatsApp at +91 788 806 0231.

Information we collect

We collect information you choose to provide when you use the ordering flow or contact us:

  • Your name, phone number, and delivery address when you save an order.
  • The products, weights, quantities, and prices included in your order.
  • The message and contact details you choose to share in a WhatsApp or email enquiry.
  • A browser-generated submission key used to prevent the same order from being saved twice.

This website does not collect payment card details. Payment is arranged separately with KAYAM SPICES after an order is confirmed.

Information stored in your browser

We use browser storage to keep your cart between visits and to show your order confirmation in the current browser tab:

  • Local storage keeps your cart and a checkout submission key on your device.
  • Session storage keeps a temporary order summary for the confirmation page and remembers whether the announcement bar was dismissed in that session.

These are essential site functions, not advertising or analytics tools. You can clear this data through your browser settings. Clearing it may remove your cart or prevent an order summary from being displayed again in that tab.

How we use your information

  • To save and review the order you submit.
  • To confirm product availability, delivery arrangements, delivery charges, and payment with you.
  • To respond to enquiries and provide order support.
  • To maintain order records and protect the website and ordering process from misuse.

Why we process it

We process order and enquiry information because it is needed to respond to your request and manage the ordering relationship. Where consent is required, we rely on the consent you give when you choose to send an enquiry or order.

Sharing information

Order information is stored in our Supabase-hosted database and is available to authorised KAYAM SPICES administrators. When you choose to open WhatsApp, the message you send is handled by WhatsApp under its own privacy terms. Email enquiries are handled by the email provider used for the contact address above.

We do not sell customer information. We may disclose information if required by applicable law or to protect the rights, safety, or security of customers and the business.

Retention

We keep order and enquiry records for as long as needed to manage the order, respond to questions, keep appropriate business records, and meet legal obligations. Browser data remains on your device until you clear it or your browser removes it.

Security

We use access controls and a hosted database to protect order information. No internet transmission or storage system is completely secure, so please avoid sharing unnecessary sensitive information in a message.

Your choices and rights

You may ask to access, correct, or delete information you have provided, subject to applicable law and any records we need to retain. Contact anikhindi.suchi@gmail.com or +91 788 806 0231 with your request and enough information for us to identify the relevant order or enquiry.

Third-party links

This site links to WhatsApp, email, and social or administrative services. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies. Review those policies before sharing information with them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the website or our processes change. The date at the top of this page shows the latest revision.

Contact
